Privacy Policy for HALE
Last updated: May 2026
Overview
HALE is a breathing exercise app developed by CoraCorp. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information when you use HALE.
Data We Collect
HALE does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All app preferences, session settings, and reminder times are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to any server.
Permissions
HALE requests the following permissions:
Notifications — used solely to send breathing reminders at times you set
Vibration — used for haptic feedback during breathing sessions
Third Party Services
HALE uses Google Play Billing to process premium purchases. Any transactions made through the app are handled by Google and subject to Google's Privacy Policy. CoraCorp does not store or have access to your payment information.
Children's Privacy
HALE does not knowingly collect any data from users of any age. The app is suitable for general audiences.
Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page.
